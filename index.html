Safety Features

Safety Is Not Optional. It's Our Priority.

Safety features listed in the A T Elevator brochure. Availability depends on the elevator model and configuration you choose; we confirm the exact list in your quotation.

Full details in the brochure

  • Infrared Light Curtain

    Full-height crossing light beams detect small obstructions in the door path and stop the doors from closing.

  • Overload Sensor

    Audio-visual indication on overload; doors stay open until the excess load is removed.

  • Slot-less Door Sills

    Groove-less stainless steel sills leave no slots for dust to collect and obstruct the doors.

  • Emergency Light & Fan

    Switches to battery backup on power failure; the fan keeps running for at least 20 minutes.

  • Alarm & Intercom

    Battery-backed emergency alarm and push-to-talk link to the lobby during a power failure.

  • Emergency Rescue

    On power failure, passengers can use the emergency rescue button on the car panel.

  • Over-travel Protection

    Power to the motor is cut and the brake applied if the car travels beyond the top or bottom floor.

  • Overspeed Governor

    If the car exceeds rated speed downward, the governor trips the safety gear onto the guide rails.

  • PU Buffers

    Long-lasting, weather-resistant polyurethane buffers for dependable protection at the pit.

  • Car Fan Auto-Sleep

    The car fan switches off automatically when the lift is idle, saving energy.

  • Voice Announcer

    Announces next floor, direction and door status, with optional music.

  • V3F Drive & Door Operator

    Microprocessor-based variable voltage, variable frequency drive for faster, smoother door operation.
