Safety Features

Safety Is Not Optional. It's Our Priority.

Safety features available across the A T Elevator range. The exact list depends on the model and configuration you choose and is confirmed in your quotation.

  • Infrared Light Curtain

    Full-height crossing light beams detect the smallest obstruction in the door path and stop the doors from closing.

  • Overload Sensor

    Audio-visual indication on overload; doors stay open until the excess load is removed.

  • Slot-less Door Sills

    Groove-less stainless steel sills match the car and leave no slots for dust to collect and obstruct the doors.

  • Car Emergency Light & Fan

    Switches automatically to battery backup on power failure; the fan keeps running for at least 20 minutes.

  • Emergency Alarm & Push-to-Talk

    Self-charging, battery-backed alarm and intercom link to the lobby during an emergency or power failure.

  • Emergency Rescue Button

    On power failure, passengers can use the emergency rescue button on the car panel.

  • Over-travel Protection

    Power to the controller and motor is cut and the brake applied if the car moves beyond the top or bottom floor.

  • Overspeed Governor

    If the car exceeds rated speed downward, the governor trips the safety gear onto the guide rails and stops the car.

  • PU Buffers

    Long-lasting, weather-resistant polyurethane buffers for dependable protection at the pit.

  • Car Fan Auto-Sleep

    The car fan switches off automatically when the lift is idle, saving energy.

  • Voice Announcer with Music

    Announces next floor, direction and door status with a natural electronic voice.

  • V3F Drive & Door Operator

    Microprocessor-based variable voltage, variable frequency drive with encoder feedback for faster, smoother door operation and shorter waiting time.
